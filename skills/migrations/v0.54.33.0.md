# v0.54.33.0: legacy maintenance lanes skip on a managed brain

Managed brains require no operator action: background scheduling skips legacy
maintenance lanes automatically once the coordinator owns writes. If the queue
already holds dead-lettered autopilot-cycle, global-maintenance, extract, or
extract-atoms-drain jobs from before this version, they are inert — requeue or
discard them at your discretion; nothing rewrites their rows.

1. Run `gbrain upgrade` on every host that runs `gbrain autopilot` or a minion
   worker against the managed brain. Mixed-version fleets are safe: an older
   autopilot may still dispatch a legacy lane, and an upgraded worker resolves
   the queued job as skipped rather than dead-lettering it.
2. No configuration keys change. Sync freshness jobs are unaffected; managed
   sync remains the coordinator's write path.
