- The dream paid-loop breaker now also counts dead `dream:patterns:`/`dream:synth-v2:` submissions per phase and source under a `dream:phase:<phase>:<source>` key, so a content-hashed key family that re-keys every cycle (for example a `patterns` run that keeps dying on `prompt_too_long`) trips once after the usual 3 deaths in 24h instead of looping forever on fresh keys. Reset with `gbrain dream reset-key 'dream:phase:<phase>:<source>'`; resetting an individual submission key also stops its deaths counting toward the phase key.
