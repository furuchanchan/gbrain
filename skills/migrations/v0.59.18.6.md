# Upgrade to v0.59.18.6: complete resumable withdrawal discovery

This release finishes the resumable withdrawal-discovery design begun in
v0.59.18.5: facts provenance now scans in bounded batches, both drift
phases stream by write timestamp so concurrent writes during a paused plan
are still covered, and retained plans expire or are garbage-collected when
their source disappears. The `fact_withdrawal_discovery` table is unchanged;
no new services, connectors, or external calls are introduced.

1. **Prepare a maintenance window.** Stop old CLI, resident, sync and worker
   processes for this brain. Take and verify an engine-appropriate full
   backup. Mixed old/new writers are not a supported upgrade procedure.

2. **Upgrade and confirm.** Run `gbrain upgrade --no-autopilot-install`,
   then confirm `gbrain --version` reports v0.59.18.6 or newer. If the binary
   upgraded but schema work did not complete, apply
   `gbrain apply-migrations --yes --no-autopilot-install` after the backup
   and quiescence checks.

3. **Retry a previously refused or wedged withdrawal.** A `forget` over a
   large facts-heavy source now resumes bounded progress across retries
   instead of re-running a whole-source provenance query against a statement
   timeout. A plan retained from a paused attempt restarts any drift window
   that a concurrent write could have entered during the pause.

4. **Expect resumable receipts, not partial withdrawals.** A pending receipt
   means discovery committed no ledger, expiry, revision, or chunk changes;
   the withdrawal publishes atomically only after a final coverage probe
   finds every drift window empty.

5. **Verify.** `SELECT * FROM fact_withdrawal_discovery` shows at most one
   in-flight plan per `(source_id, claims_key)`; rows idle over an hour, or
   whose source was deleted, are collected on the next entry.

If a step fails, preserve the backup and diagnostic output and follow the
linked recovery guide. Do not run a destructive reset or force a revision
to make the status turn green.
