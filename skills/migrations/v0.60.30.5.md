# v0.60.30.5 — dream patterns pages carry a raw-trace exemption (#5884)

Since the patterns phase started stamping `dream_generated`, its pages fell
under doctor's `raw_provenance` invariant without ever recording a trace —
and a manual `raw_source`/`raw_trace_exempt` stamp was wiped by the next
patterns run's `put_page`.

The stamp itself now declares the exemption on every run:

    raw_trace_exempt: true
    raw_trace_exempt_reason: "derived from the reflection corpus; raw
      traces live on the cited reflection pages"

Applied on both write lanes (unmanaged `stampDreamProvenance` and managed
`stampMaintenancePage`). `stampMaintenancePage` and `stampDreamProvenance`
gained an optional exempt-reason parameter; other callers are unchanged.
