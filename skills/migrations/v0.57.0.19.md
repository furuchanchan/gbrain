# v0.57.0.19 — conversation parser honors `timezone:` frontmatter

`page.frontmatter.timezone` (IANA name, e.g. `Asia/Tokyo`) was previously
read only to suppress the `utc_assumed_with_warn` notice. Wall-clock
captures were still emitted as `<date>T<hh>:<mm>:00Z` — off by the zone
offset for segment timestamps, fact `valid_from`, and `--since` filters.

Now, for `utc_assumed_with_warn` patterns only, captures are interpreted
in the declared zone and converted to UTC. `inline_utc` patterns already
carry real UTC and are untouched. An unrecognized zone name degrades to
the old assume-UTC behavior instead of failing the parse.

No action required.
