# v0.60.27.x — DeepSeek pricing refresh

If your dream judge or chat model id is `deepseek:deepseek-flash`, the budget
meter now prices it directly (previously it warned `BUDGET_METER_NO_PRIZING`
and metered at the Sonnet fallback rate). Configurations still on the legacy
`deepseek-v4-flash` or `deepseek-v4-pro` ids keep working — their rates were
updated to current vendor pricing — but expect metered DeepSeek spend figures
to rise versus earlier versions, since the vendor's current rates are higher.
