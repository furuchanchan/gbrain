# Upgrade to 0.60.31.11: pack sketch regexes no longer mistype wikilinks

A new `link_types[].inference.markdown_links` manifest field (default:
the rule participates everywhere) opts a pack regex out of markdown
wikilink typing. The shipped `gbrain-base` and `gbrain-base-v2` packs
mark their four NER-only sketch verbs (founded/works_at/invested_in/
advises) `markdown_links: false`, and on `meeting` pages only
`page_type: 'meeting'`-bound pack rules may pre-empt the attendance
prior.

Pages whose links were mistyped by the sketch regexes are re-typed on
the next `gbrain extract --stale` (the link extractor watermark moved to
2026-10-02). No action is required; named-entity extraction
(`extract-ner`) keeps the same patterns.
