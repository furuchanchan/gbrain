# Migrating to v0.60.25.1

No schema changes and no config moves are required.

## What changed for you

- **A new repair kind restores facts lost to the fenceless-page expiry bug.** If your brain ran a version before the projection fix while managed pages without a `## Facts` fence were written, `cli:`-sourced facts (conversation extraction) may have been silently expired. Preview the damage and restore it after you agree:
  ```bash
  gbrain repair expired-facts            # preview
  gbrain repair expired-facts --apply    # re-activate them
  ```
  Facts you explicitly forgot stay expired — the repair verifies each candidate against the durable withdrawal record.
