# v0.60.29.0

## brain_score timeline component is graded by pack primitive (#5828)

`gbrain doctor`'s `brain_score` timeline component (15 points) now measures
timeline coverage over pages whose type's primitive is `entity` or
`temporal` in your active schema pack. Document types (`note`, `writing`,
`guide`, `concept`, `media`, `synthesis`, …) no longer dilute the
denominator — a document-heavy brain that previously read ~4/15 on this
component now reflects its actual event coverage.

- Types your pack does not declare are still graded (unchanged behaviour).
- A brain with zero graded pages gets the vacuous-truth full marks.
- If the pack cannot be resolved, the component falls back to the
  previous all-linkable-pages denominator rather than failing.
- The `no_orphans` and `links` components, and the entity-scoped
  `timeline_coverage` metric, are unchanged.

No action needed — scores recompute on the next `gbrain doctor` /
`get_health` run. If you intentionally relied on documents counting
toward this component, declare their type with an `entity` or `temporal`
primitive in your pack (or note that undeclared types remain graded).
