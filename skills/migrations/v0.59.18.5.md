# Upgrade to v0.59.18.5: resumable withdrawal discovery

This release makes bounded withdrawal discovery resumable across invocations
and adds the `fact_withdrawal_discovery` plan table (migration in the normal
schema chain). It adds no new background services, installs no connectors, and
authorizes no external calls beyond the work the operator already scheduled.

1. **Prepare a maintenance window.** Stop old CLI, resident, sync and worker
   processes for this brain. Take and verify an engine-appropriate full
   backup. Mixed old/new writers are not a supported upgrade procedure.

2. **Upgrade and confirm.** Run `gbrain upgrade --no-autopilot-install`,
   then confirm `gbrain --version` reports v0.59.18.5 or newer. If the binary
   upgraded but schema work did not complete, apply
   `gbrain apply-migrations --yes --no-autopilot-install` after the backup
   and quiescence checks.

3. **Retry a previously refused withdrawal.** A `forget` over a source that
   exhausted the discovery scan budget now returns a `withdrawal_pending`
   receipt and resumes committed progress on the next attempt — retry the
   same operation rather than splitting or deleting the source.

4. **Expect resumable receipts, not partial withdrawals.** A pending receipt
   means discovery committed no ledger, expiry, revision, or chunk changes;
   the retained plan resumes from its cursor and the withdrawal publishes
   atomically only when the full target set is verified.

5. **Verify.** `SELECT * FROM fact_withdrawal_discovery` shows at most one
   in-flight plan per `(source_id, claims_key)`; rows older than one hour are
   garbage-collected on completed scans.

If a step fails, preserve the backup and diagnostic output and follow the
linked recovery guide. Do not run a destructive reset or force a revision
to make the status turn green.
