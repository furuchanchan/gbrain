# Upgrade to v0.60.32.10: managed atoms stamp the final source_hash

No migration steps — this release fixes one provenance stamp.

- Atoms extracted on a managed brain now carry the final `source_hash`
  instead of `pending:<hash16>` forever. Managed extraction's done-signal
  was always the durable managed-atoms checkpoint; the provisional marker
  was never flipped (#5938).
- Atoms already stamped `pending:<hash16>` are not rewritten. They keep
  resolving through the doctor check's `pending:` strip, and a
  re-extraction of their source mints replacements at the final hash.
- No flip of stored rows is needed or performed — a database-only flip
  would diverge the stored frontmatter from the canonical atom file and
  read as an uncoordinated local edit on the next write.

No paid work, no permissions, no schema changes. `gbrain upgrade` applies it.
