# v0.60.27.8

- Pages full of gbrain's own `## Facts`/`## Takes` tables no longer get flagged `markup_heavy` by `content_sanity`. The prose heuristic now excludes gbrain-managed fences (like code) instead of counting their pipe rows as markup; externally-authored table markup still counts. If doctor `flagged_pages` warned about fence-heavy entity pages, the flags stop on the next write/re-scan. No action needed.
