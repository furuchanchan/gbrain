# v0.58.1.44 — worker-backed single-source sync delivers large deferrals

`gbrain sync --source <id>` on a Postgres-backed brain used to drop the
intrinsic >100-file `large_sync` deferral on the floor — no backfill job,
no drain hint — leaving fresh chunks unembedded until a manual
`gbrain embed --stale`. The single-source gate now mirrors `--all`: when
the worker surface exists, the deferral reaches the backfill submitter and
fresh chunks get embedded without manual repair.

No schema or data migration; nothing re-imports or re-embeds. No action
needed.
