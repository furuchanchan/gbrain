# Upgrade to v0.60.32.1: keyword-only answers recover their honest grade

No migration steps — this release changes one grade the `query` op reports,
not what it stores or sends.

- On a brain running without embeddings or a reranker, a `query` whose
  returned top five includes a chunk covering the asked entity and attribute
  now reports `crag.confidence: moderate` with reason
  `keyword_relaxed_rescued` instead of `weak` / `keyword_relaxed_top`.
- Anything keyed on `reason` gains one additive value; the set is
  additive-only and `weak`/`moderate`/`strong` levels are unchanged.
- Unanswerable questions still grade `weak`; the abstention story from
  v0.60.32.0 is preserved (measured: 0/120 above weak on the A4 world).

No paid work, no permissions, no schema changes. `gbrain upgrade` applies it.
